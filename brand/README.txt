BAAKI BUILD by AAYAMIK - final logo
Finalised 31 Oct 2026

THE IDEA
A location pin (material near you) holding a small brick wall. One mustard
brick slides out of its row and past the pin's edge: leftover material
leaving one site for another.

FILES
SVG/        Vector masters. Lettering is outlined (no font needed).
PNG/        Transparent, large (mark 1600 px, horizontal 3000 px, stacked 2000 px).
App-Icons/  Brick (red tile, as in the app) and Green (forest tile) sets.

Colourways
  Brick       brick-red pin, mustard brick, green lettering - on cream or white (main version)
  Green       forest-green pin, mustard brick, green lettering - on cream or white
  Cream       cream pin, mustard brick, cream lettering - on forest green or brick red
  Green-Mono  one colour, forest green - print, stamps, single-colour jobs
  White       one colour - over photos

COLOURS (sampled from the app poster)
  Forest green  #3E5A47
  Brick red     #B33A1E
  Mustard       #E2B33C
  Cream         #FAF6F1
  No black anywhere.

TYPE
  Plus Jakarta Sans: BAAKI BUILD in ExtraBold (800), BY AAYAMIK in SemiBold (600), widely spaced.

APP ICONS (each set)
  icon-512.png, icon-192.png  web app manifest, purpose "any"
  maskable-512.png            web app manifest, purpose "maskable" (Android crops it)
  apple-touch-icon.png        180 px, iPhone / iPad home screen
  play-store-512.png          Google Play listing icon
  favicon.ico, favicon-32/16  browser tab (fewer, larger bricks so they stay clear when small)
  icon.svg                    1024 px vector master of the icon

RULES
  - Keep clear space around the logo of at least the pin's width at its widest / 3.
  - Smallest sizes: mark 16 px (use the favicon files); horizontal logo 140 px wide.
  - Don't recolour outside the colourways, stretch, or move the bricks.
  - The mustard brick always stays mustard, except in the one-colour versions.
