EVERYTHING IT DOES

Every step of a sale, screen by screen.

Sample screens with sample listings, from the Android, iPhone and web app.

01 · POST A LISTING

Leftover stock online in two minutes, at no cost.

  • Up to six photos, the category, quantity and unit, condition and the total price
  • The unlock fee buyers will pay is shown as you type the price
  • Public area for everyone; exact pickup address kept private, with a GPS pin
  • Photos are checked on the phone before upload and again on the server
  • A listing goes live as soon as one photo passes

02 · FIND MATERIAL NEARBY

Search by material and city, see the real price up front.

  • Sixteen categories, from cement and TMT bars to sanitary and paint
  • Search across titles, details and localities; filter by city
  • Price, quantity, condition and the approximate area on every listing
  • The unlock fee is shown on each card, before anyone pays
  • Report a listing for a wrong price, contact details or a fake

03 · UNLOCK THE CONTACT

One small payment, then a direct call.

  • Pay by UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay
  • The seller’s phone and exact address appear the moment payment clears
  • Call and Directions buttons, and every unlock saved under “Unlocked”
  • Refund within seven days if the material was already sold, or after 48 hours without a reply
  • The contact is held on the server, so no app trick reveals it early

04 · FOR THE SELLER

See who is interested, and call them back.

  • Every buyer who unlocked a listing, with their name and number
  • Edit a listing, mark it sold, or hide it for later
  • Unlock counts and photo checks shown for each listing
  • No calls from people who are only browsing

FEES

Free to list. A small fee to unlock.

Sellers never pay. A buyer pays once per listing to see the seller’s phone and exact pickup address, then the two deal directly. The material is paid for at pickup, between buyer and seller.
