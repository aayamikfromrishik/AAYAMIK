KD WEBSITE: HOW TO UPDATE THE WEBSITE
=====================================

Whatever is in this folder appears on karukritdesigns.com.
Save your changes here. About 10 to 15 minutes later the website shows them.
You do not need to open any program.

THE RULES
- A number at the start of a name sets the order: "01 Sharma Residence", "02 Gill Residence".
- The rest of the name is what the website shows. Write it the way you want it to read.
- Anything whose name starts with _ (underscore) is ignored. Use that for examples and notes.
- Photos: JPG or PNG, straight from the camera or phone is fine. The website resizes them.
- Text files: open with Notepad, change the text after the colon, save.
- Videos larger than 80 MB are skipped. Export a smaller version, or put a YouTube link in info.txt.

WHAT GOES WHERE
1 Home
    Sketches     Hand sketches, paper removed (transparent PNG), 8 to 12 of them.
                 Name them "01 Sharma Residence.png". The colour render of the same house
                 goes beside it as "01 Sharma Residence render.jpg".
    3D Model     One house exported from SketchUp as glTF (.glb). It replaces the study house
                 that draws itself on the home page.
2 About          Your portrait (any .jpg) and story.txt.
3 Team           One photo per person, named "01 Kuljeet - Site architect.jpg"
                 (number, name, dash, role). team.txt holds the one sentence about the studio.
4 Philosophy     sentences.txt: the three sentences.
5 Projects       One folder per category, one folder per project inside it.
                 Copy the _Example folder, rename it to the project's name, fill in info.txt.
                 Completed project:  elevation.jpg, and folders "Site photographs", "Interiors", "Drawings".
                 Ongoing project:    render.jpg, and "Site photos" with a folder per stage
                                     ("Stage 4"), photos named with the date first:
                                     "2026-10-30 north wall.jpg".
                 Only presentation drawings, never working drawings with dimensions.
6 Contact        details.txt

IF SOMETHING DOES NOT APPEAR
Look at _website-log.txt in this folder. It says when the website last updated and lists
anything it could not use.
